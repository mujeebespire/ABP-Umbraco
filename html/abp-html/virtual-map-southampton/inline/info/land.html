Strategic Land Reserve

The Strategic Land Reserve spans over 500 acres, much of which was reclaimed using material from various dredging operations in Southampton Water. This area also includes land situated behind the reclaimed land, which is currently used primarily for agriculture.

As we look ahead to the future development of the port estate, we are committed to exploring opportunities that deliver long-term, sustainable value—supporting economic growth, strengthening community connections, and enhancing environmental resilience.
