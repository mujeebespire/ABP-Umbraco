Cruise Parking

We offer car parking on site. For your convenience, you can park your car and walk straight to the cruise terminal for a hassle free start to your holiday.

ABParking is the only official cruise car parking provider with on-site car parks located within the secure port estate for Celebrity Cruises, Disney Cruise Line, Fred. Olsen Cruise Lines, MSC Cruises, Norwegian Cruise Line, Oceania Cruises, Regent Seven Seas Cruises, Royal Caribbean International and Silversea Cruises.

ABParking have multiple cruise terminal car parks around City Cruise Terminal and Horizon Cruise Terminal; Green, Blue, Pink and Red. You will be allocated a car parking space within one of these car parks at the time of booking depending on which terminal you are sailing from. Blue Badge car parks are also available at both City and Horizon Cruise Terminal.

To park at QEII, Ocean or Mayflower Cruise terminals please contact CPS - www.cruiseparking.co.uk/ports/southampton/
